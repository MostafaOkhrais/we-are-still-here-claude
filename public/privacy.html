Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal data — clearly and transparently. Last updated: 12 September 2026.

Who we are and what this covers

We Are Still Here | Baqoun is a Palestinian student-led initiative supporting Gaza's university students. This policy applies to this website: its contact and subscription forms and content management.

Data we collect

Contact form: name, email, subject, and message. Newsletter: email (name optional). To prevent abuse we keep an encrypted fingerprint of the connection address (never the address itself) plus a truncated browser identifier.

Why we use it

To reply to your inquiries, send the newsletter, protect forms from spam, and administer the site. No advertising, no data sales, no sharing with any third party.

Cookies and technologies

No tracking or analytics. Strictly necessary only: a security cookie from our infrastructure provider telling humans from bots (expires in about half an hour), your language choice stored in your browser, and the admin login session in session memory (admins only, cleared when the browser closes). Loading fonts exposes your connection address to their provider under its policy.

Who processes data

Our secure database and hosting infrastructure, and authorized initiative team members only. External links (donations, shop, WhatsApp, social platforms) leave our site and follow their own policies.

Retention and your rights

We keep data only as long as needed for its purpose. You may access, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing us below, and we will act on your request without delay.

Policy updates

We may update this page when our practices change, with the update date shown above. Continued use of the site means accepting the current version.
