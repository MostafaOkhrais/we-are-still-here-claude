باقون We Are Still Here

We Are Still Here | Baqoun was founded on the firm conviction that education must continue, and that students should not lose their futures due to circumstances beyond their control. It began in April 2024 as voluntary academic support initiated by Professor Zahid Pranjol for Gaza's students, and evolved into a student-led and community-based initiative supporting and empowering university students.
