Team

One team working across Gaza and the United Kingdom

The initiative operates through a team of ~50 members and volunteers — ~30 in Gaza and ~20 in the UK — alongside a network of academics, trainers, volunteers, and supporters, including ~10 trainers and academics delivering educational and training programs. Departments work interconnectedly, with coordination ensuring no duplication and full integration.

Functional areas

Department-level overview only, without personal data.

Education & Academic Programs

Designing educational programs, coordinating with academics and trainers, and following up students, attendance, and evaluation.

Student Support & Scholarships

Supporting students in accessing scholarships and opportunities, and verifying cases and needs.

Public Relations

Building relationships with institutions, universities, and organizations, and engaging potential partners.

Social Media

Managing the digital presence, preparing content, announcing programs, and documenting achievements.

Design

Designing visual materials and publications, and supporting campaigns and cultural projects.

Logistics

Organizing operational needs, logistical coordination of events, and supporting departments in delivery.
