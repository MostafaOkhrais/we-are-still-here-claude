The story of Baqoun: from voluntary support to a community initiative

In April 2024, Professor Zahid Pranjol began offering voluntary academic support to students in Gaza, helping them to continue learning despite the difficult circumstances. As needs grew and the scope of the work widened, these efforts developed into a student-led, community-based initiative named We Are Still Here | Baqoun. It began with the aim of helping students to continue their education, and has since broadened to empower Palestinian young people and to strengthen their capacity for initiative, leadership and community participation.

Vision

To become a trusted and leading platform for supporting and empowering Palestinian university students: a space that gives them access to the resources, opportunities, knowledge and support they need to continue learning, develop their abilities, strengthen their participation and leadership, and fulfil their ambitions despite exceptional circumstances.

Mission

To support and empower Palestinian university students and to protect their right to education and development, through educational and training programmes, student support, scholarships and opportunities, cultural, literary and artistic initiatives, and academic and community partnerships, all designed around students' actual needs and direct experiences.

Values

Our core values

Human Dignity

Treating every student as a rights-holder and an active participant in their community.

Student-Centredness

Designing programmes and decisions on the basis of students' needs and experiences.

Empowerment

Moving beyond direct support to build knowledge, skills and leadership.

Accountability

A commitment to accuracy, transparency and responsibility for decisions and resources.

Collaboration

Building a connected network of students, volunteers, academics, institutions and partners.

Adaptability

Adapting our working methods to changing and exceptional circumstances.

Participation

Involving the field team and stakeholders in developing programmes and decisions.

Programmes

Programmes and activities

1English Conversation Programme

English Conversation Programme

For university students whose studies have been affected by current circumstances, the programme strengthens their use of English in academic, professional and everyday communication. It is delivered through weekly online sessions led by English-speaking volunteers and trainers, is ongoing, and has so far benefited dozens of students.

2Comprehensive Academic Programme

Comprehensive Multi-Disciplinary Academic Programme

A programme for university students across a range of disciplines, delivered through courses taught by academics, university lecturers and trainers from inside and outside Gaza. Each lecturer decides the duration and content of their course to suit their field. Admission normally follows these stages: announcement of the course, applications and screening, interviews, and selection on the basis of seriousness, need and suitability, followed by teaching, attendance monitoring and evaluation.

3Student Support

Student Support and Financial Assistance

Financial assistance for students facing exceptional needs, awarded after an assessment of each student's circumstances. Cases are managed and documented by the field team in Gaza in coordination with the initiative's leadership, with direct oversight by Professor Zahid Pranjol for major financial decisions. To date, the initiative has handled hundreds of cases.

4Literary and Artistic

Literary and Artistic Projects

Spaces for students to express their experiences, produce literary and artistic work, and share their voices with a wider audience. These efforts include two books published by Daraja Press, an exhibition at Gallery North, and cultural events that have reached an international audience in the United Kingdom.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is We Are Still Here | Baqoun?

It is a Palestinian student-led, community-based initiative that supports and empowers university students in Gaza. It began in April 2024, when Professor Zahid Pranjol started offering voluntary academic support to students in Gaza.

Who leads the initiative?

Professor Zahid Pranjol is the Founder and Director, and Obay Jouda is the Gaza Project Lead. The initiative has around 50 members and volunteers: around 30 in Gaza and around 20 in the United Kingdom.

Which programmes does the initiative run?

Four programmes: the English Conversation Programme, the Comprehensive Multi-Disciplinary Academic Programme, Student Support and Financial Assistance, and Literary and Artistic Projects.

How can I support the initiative?

You can donate through our Chuffed fundraising page, or buy Teemill merchandise and our two books published by Daraja Press from the Shop page. Proceeds support student programmes in Gaza.

How can I contact the initiative?

Use the form on the Contact page or write to info@wearestillhere.uk. We aim to reply within 24 to 48 hours.

Where does the initiative work?

The team works across Gaza and the United Kingdom, with Palestinian field leadership in Gaza under the general supervision of the Founder.
