The story of Baqoun: from voluntary support to a community initiative

In April 2024, Professor Zahid Pranjol initiated voluntary academic support for Gaza's students, helping them continue learning despite difficult circumstances. As needs grew and the scope widened, these efforts evolved into a student-led and community-based initiative named We Are Still Here | Baqoun — which began with the aim of supporting students to continue their education, then broadened to empowering Palestinian youth and strengthening their capacity for initiative, leadership, and community participation.

Vision

To become a trusted, leading platform supporting and empowering Palestinian university students — a space giving them access to the resources, opportunities, knowledge, and support needed to continue learning, develop their capacities, strengthen participation and leadership, and achieve ambitions despite exceptional circumstances.

Mission

To support and empower Palestinian university students and protect their right to education and development, through educational and training programs, student support, scholarships and opportunities, cultural, literary and artistic initiatives, and academic and community partnerships — designed on the basis of students' actual needs and direct experiences.

Values

Involving the field team and stakeholders in developing programs.

Programs

Programs and activities

1English Conversation Program

English Conversation Program

Serves university students whose academic journeys have been affected, strengthening their English for academic, professional, and communicative contexts. Weekly online sessions, ongoing, dozens served to date, via English-speaking volunteers and trainers.

2Comprehensive Academic Program

Comprehensive Multi-Disciplinary Academic Program

Serves students across disciplines via courses by academics, lecturers, and trainers from inside Gaza and abroad. Each lecturer sets duration and content to suit specialty; admission via announcement, applications, screening, interviews, selection by seriousness, need, and fit — then teaching, attendance follow-up, and evaluation.

3Student Support

Student Financial Support & Assistance

Financial assistance for students facing exceptional needs, based on assessment of need. Managed and documented by the field team in Gaza in coordination with leadership, under direct supervision of Professor Zahid Pranjol for key financial decisions. Hundreds of cases provided to date.

4Literary & Artistic

Literary and Artistic Projects

Spaces for students to express their experiences, produce literary and artistic works, and reach wider audiences — including two Daraja Press books, a Gallery North exhibition, and cultural events reaching an international audience in the UK.
